12 October, for my LOML

Happy birthday, Felicia

I write code for a living. This is the first thing I ever wrote with only one reader in mind, and it is you.

Where it began

I came for a number and found my person

You said no

We were in SHS and I went after you, only for your number. You turned me down. Then you turned me down again.

I sent my juniors

I even sent them to ask for me. You still said no, and I still did not stop.

My last move

I told myself this one would be the last. At an inter-schools game, I made it, and you gave me the number. Best last move I ever made.

The first day

The day you first came to my place

The room felt different once you were in it. I did not know that ordinary day would turn into the one I keep going back to. You smiled at the camera like you had always belonged there, and you were right.

Quiet moments

No plans, only you

These are my favourite kind of moments. You close by, nothing to prove, and me feeling like nothing else needed to happen.

In the sun

You, in blue, smiling at something I will never fully know

Whatever it was, I hope I get to be the reason next time.

Us, moving

Eleven seconds I would watch every day

Press play. This one is the two of us, exactly as we are.

Look at you

Beautiful, strong, and completely yourself

Today the world gets to celebrate you. I get to be the one standing closest.

Why you

Six things I love about you

  • Your smile

    It ends my worst days without asking.

  • Your calm

    Near you, everything slows to a speed I can live at.

  • Your strength

    You carry more than you show, and you still show up for me.

  • Being understood

    You see me without needing me to explain.

  • Your presence

    Some people fill a room. You make it feel like home.

  • Choosing me

    Out of everyone, you stayed. I do not take that lightly.

To Felicia, my LOML,

You are all I have, and somehow that has never felt like too little. With you I have a person to come home to, a reason to build, and a reason to be better than I was yesterday.

I followed you in school for a number, and you turned me down again and again. I told myself it was my last move, and at that inter-schools game you finally gave it to me. Everything good since then started with that one yes.

I know how to make machines do what I ask. I do not always know how to say all of this out loud, so I built it instead. Every line of this page is a way of saying I love you.

Thank you for the days you spend with me, for your patience on the hard ones, and for the way you look at me when you think I am not watching.

Today is yours. I hope it is gentle, loud, and full of everything you deserve. And on every day after it, I will keep choosing you.

Happy birthday, my love.
Yours, always,
John
